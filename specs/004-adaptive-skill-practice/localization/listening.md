# Listening practice localization

`practice.listening.title`, `practice.listening.instruction`, `practice.listening.description`, `practice.listening.replay`, and `practice.listening.slowReplay` are required in fa/en/de. Before feedback or reveal the transcript and sentence translation must not be rendered or exposed through accessibility. The learner answer uses its answer-language direction independently of the surrounding app direction. Leaving the screen cancels the model request and invokes the injected playback stop boundary.
